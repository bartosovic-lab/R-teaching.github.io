## Data visualization and statistical analysis lab

#### Day 1 · Interactive tutorial: *From a table to a biological story*

**[⬇ Download the Day 1 materials (zip, < 1 MB)](../Notebooks/Statistics_lab_2026.zip)**

The zip unpacks to one folder, `Statistics_lab_2026`, containing:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Statistics_lab.Rproj` | The RStudio project. Open this first |
| `day1.Rmd` | The interactive tutorial |
| `helpers.R`, `setup.R` | Support code; `setup.R` installs the packages you need |
| `data/mmse_small.csv`, `data/neurites.csv` | The teaching data (with `data/README.md`) |
| `Launch_day1.command`, `Launch_day1.bat` | Double-click launchers for Mac and Windows |
| `README.md` | These instructions |

**Before the workshop:** install [R](https://cran.r-project.org) and then [RStudio Desktop](https://posit.co/download/rstudio-desktop/). Both are free. Unzip the download and keep the folder together.

**To launch the tutorial**, either

- **A. Double-click** `Launch_day1.command` (Mac) or `Launch_day1.bat` (Windows). The first time, macOS may ask you to right-click → Open. A black window appears and your browser opens the tutorial. Keep the black window open while you work; close it to stop.
- **B. In RStudio:** double-click `Statistics_lab.Rproj`. In the Console type `source("setup.R")` and press Enter. Then open `day1.Rmd` and click **Run Document** at the top of the editor. The tutorial opens in a window; use **Show in new window** for full size.

Everything you type is remembered by your browser. At the end of the tutorial, type your name and click **Download my work** to save all your code and answers as one HTML file.
