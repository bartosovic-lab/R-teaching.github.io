2. What are these data?

The observations come from the OASIS-2 longitudinal study. The previous course file contained 336 visits from 136 people. This version keeps the first visit of each of those 136 participants, preserving the original course values and rounding. We restored the participant IDs from the original source.

The two labels, Demented and Nondemented, are the source study’s longitudinal group classifications. Participants in its Converted group were already absent from the previous course file and remain excluded. This selection limits generalisation. These are observational data, not a randomised treatment experiment. Comparing MMSE across related clinical groups does not independently validate a diagnostic test.

6. Transfer: multiple testing — independent task

Imagine three planned treatment-versus-control comparisons in one experiment. These three questions form a family of tests. The p-values below are made up for this arithmetic example; they are not results from our dementia dataset.

If we try many tests and report only the smallest p-value, we hide the number of opportunities to find an apparently unusual result. Bonferroni adjustment accounts for how many tests we are considering together: multiply each p-value by the number of tests and cap the result at 1.
